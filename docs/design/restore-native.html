What a native restore creates
ClickHouse restores the selected databases from the backup itself, with their tables, views, materialized views and dictionaries as they were defined. An object that could reach outside the target, or that Olympos cannot read with certainty, is left out with the reason above. Left out objects are re-applied by hand after the restore. A Replicated table gets a Keeper path of its own (/olympos-restore/<request id>/<db>/<table>), so it never joins the source's replicas.
